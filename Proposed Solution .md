# FieldOS

## Your AI Co-Worker for Field Operations.

(Gnani AI Internship Challenge) (Voice-first AI) (Multilingual) (Agentic workflows)

**Project category\:** AI-powered field workforce productivity and operational intelligence platform.

## 1\. Project overview

**FieldOS is an AI-powered, multilingual field operations platform that acts as a digital co-worker for field technicians, maintenance engineers, inspectors and other field personnel.**

It enables field workers to interact with their operational environment using natural voice conversations instead of relying on traditional manual reporting and documentation.

Powered by Gnani AI's speech technologies and an intelligent reasoning layer, FieldOS converts unstructured voice inputs into structured information, identifies operational issues, asks relevant follow-up questions, generates reports and initiates actionable workflows.

It also connects field workers with supervisors through a centralized dashboard, enabling better coordination, visibility and task management.

**The core vision is to transform how field workers communicate, document their work and execute operational tasks.**

---

## 2\. Problem statement

Field workers operate in environments where traditional digital tools are often inconvenient, time-consuming or difficult to access.

Some major challenges include\:

1. **Manual documentation\:** Workers spend considerable time filling out forms and preparing reports.
2. **Communication barriers\:** Workers may naturally communicate in regional languages, while organizations require standardized reports.
3. **Information loss\:** Important technical details can be forgotten or omitted during manual reporting.
4. **Operational delays\:** Supervisors may receive information late, delaying maintenance and issue resolution.
5. **Disconnected workflows\:** Reporting, task assignment and progress tracking often happen through separate tools.
6. **Difficult working environments\:** Noisy surroundings, gloves and limited access to devices make conventional interfaces inconvenient.

There is a need for a system that makes operational reporting more accessible, intelligent and actionable.

## 3\. Proposed solution

FieldOS addresses these challenges through a voice-first AI co-worker that understands natural conversations and converts them into operational actions.

A field worker should be able to simply speak about a problem, inspection or completed task.

FieldOS will\:

- Understand spoken Gujarati, Hindi, English and Hinglish.
- Convert speech into text using Gnani AI.
- Extract relevant operational information.
- Identify missing or ambiguous details.
- Ask follow-up questions when necessary.
- Generate structured reports and work orders.
- Confirm important information through voice.
- Notify supervisors and update operational records.
- Track the progress of assigned tasks.

The system will minimize manual data entry while maintaining human verification for important operational decisions.
